07  BAYESIAN EDGE SENSORS
=========================
Grade: PROPOSED. Perception module, not a kernel and not the company.

What this direction is
----------------------
Hyperedges are sensors (factors). Vertices (and a small set of
global hypotheses H) are what is inferred. When an edge changes,
the *posterior* moves, not G.

Usual twins put sensors on nodes. The quantities that matter here
already live on relations: Path.link_quality, Transit, Association,
AuthorizedBy, Exposure. A likelihood on those hyperedges is AnLF
done properly: typed, relational, off the rewrite engine.

Layers (in order)
-----------------
1. Observation. The hyperedge emits a BoundaryHandle payload.
   It does not call DPO.
2. Inference. A declared factor graph. Evidence e on edge e
   updates p(H | e) over a frozen hypothesis set (residual mix,
   outage, "this patch preserves the API", claim sign). Shared
   vertices make the update global.
3. License. The posterior feeds Experiment 05 claim status.
   Tight, one sign: admit or reject. Split: hold_unresolved.
   Uninformative: outcome_unknown. Park still gates the rewrite.

Pearl stays in force. p(Y | sensors) is not p(Y | do(route)).

Do not
------
- MCMC or a variational net inside CTMC hazards.
- Let the posterior rewrite G.
- Structure-learn a new BN on every event. Topology change is
  OSAHR; Bayesian update is a fold over a declared factor graph.
- Call a weighted average of alpha a posterior.

Viability
---------
High as AnLF-v2 on small typed twins. Medium as a standalone
"Bayesian analytics" SKU (crowded unless joined to park).
Not the company. Without withhold-and-act it is another fusion UI.
