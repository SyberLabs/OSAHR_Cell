00  SHARED SUBSTRATE
====================
Grade: PROPOSED (architecture). Kernel contracts KNOWN from OSAHR 0.2.

These are the structures we can advance once, for many directions.
They are not a license to collapse domains into one schema.

1. Two twins, one park
   Mechanism twin  = OSAHR. State X = (G, B, R, Theta, Z, t, n).
                     Only DPO + clocks may rewrite G. alpha=0 is identity.
   Process twin    = SyberRuntime. Operations are source of truth;
                     artifacts are projections; verification debt is
                     the budget. See 01_digital_model.txt.
   Park / Stabilize = the same social act: low-reversibility commit.
                     Chat is not the database.

2. Junction law, not a chat answer
   Experiment 05 grammar: admit | hold_unresolved | reject | outcome_unknown.
   SyberRuntime analogue: discharged | undischarged | false-discharge | unknown.
   A Brain or generator may REQUEST a rewrite / stabilize only at hold
   (or while discharging budgeted debt). It may not promote a point
   predictor that matches oracle while the ensemble withholds.

3. Meaning in a vault, queried only at declared junctions
   PKG dual projection (markdown + YAML concept_id). Not SPARQL.
   Not a second CTMC. Promotion of a run into ontology needs a freeze.

4. Perception on the boundary
   AnLF-style tools emit BoundaryHandle payloads. They do not call
   Runtime rewrite APIs. Training / MTLF is off the event clock.

5. MCP is recon, not routing
   Tool ports are control-plane skills. They are not radio links and
   not occurrence types. One mouth. No specialist-bot swarm.

6. Freeze before confirmatory
   Checksum the grammar, vault, and analytics versions. Declare the
   seed before the run. Label claims KNOWN / MEASURED / INFERRED /
   PROPOSED.

7. Comprehension is the scarce channel
   SyberRuntime thesis: as generation cost collapses, understanding
   is the bottleneck. OSAHR thesis: predictive fidelity is not
   intervention fidelity. Same product rule: withhold-and-act.

8. Bayesian update is perception
   Hyperedges may carry factors (07). The posterior feeds claim
   status. It does not rewrite G and it does not enter clocks.

9. Two embeddings, one kernel
   Inhabitant (09): neural dynamics live in a descendant field H
   on a typed twin. Envelope (10): F is an exogenous operator;
   z lives in Theta/Z; F does not enter lambda. Do not mix. Do
   not put H in osahr/ hashes. Do not compile a Transformer into
   hyperedges.

10. Frozen F is a Feature, not a clock
    A foundation model may propose. Park licenses. Tests kill.
    Online degrees of freedom are a small z, not W.
