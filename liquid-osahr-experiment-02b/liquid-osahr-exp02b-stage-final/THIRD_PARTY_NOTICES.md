## CfC / LTC research attribution

The self-contained CfC cell is an independent research implementation based on the equations/architecture described by Hasani, Lechner and collaborators and informed by the open-source Neural Circuit Policies project. No source file from the `ncps` package is redistributed here.

Primary references:

- R. Hasani et al., *Liquid Time-constant Networks*, arXiv:2006.04439.
- R. Hasani et al., *Closed-form Continuous-time Neural Models*, arXiv:2106.13898 / Nature Machine Intelligence 2022.
- Neural Circuit Policies project: https://github.com/mlech26l/ncps

PyTorch, NumPy, SciPy and pandas are used under their respective licenses and are not vendored into this source tree.
