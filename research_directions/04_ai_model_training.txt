04  AI MODEL TRAINING
=====================
Grade: PROPOSED. MTLF stays off the OSAHR event clock.
Training is a Digital Model Feature with a checkable evaluation.

What this direction is
----------------------
Use the platform to *govern* training, not to *be* PyTorch.
A training job is an operation: data snapshot, code snapshot,
hparams, compute budget, evaluation protocol, declared seed.
Artifacts (checkpoints, metrics, cards) are projections of that
log. Residual CfC in 02B remains a hypothesis in H; this
direction does not elect alpha-star and does not train a new
CfC inside route-task hazards.

Facilitation (end to end)
-------------------------
1. Vault concepts: train_split, held_out_split, safety_eval,
   license_constraint. requires_fidelity analogue: a production
   checkpoint may not train on excluded data concepts.
2. AnLF-like tools on the cluster boundary: loss, GPU util,
   gradient-norm spikes. Payloads only. They may pause a job
   via park.disable-rule (stop-arrivals analogue). They may not
   rewrite the dataset graph.
3. Feature: launch training. Debt: undischarged eval on the
   held-out suite + a mutation/perturbation campaign on data
   order / seed where the protocol says so.
4. Claim: do(this recipe) vs do(baseline) on a declared estimand.
   Independent unit = physical/data scenario. Incomplete ensemble
   is an error.
5. admit: promote checkpoint to a named artifact (Stabilize).
   hold_unresolved: Brain may request a *legal* recipe tweak
   among vault-legal hparams (load-penalty analogue: cheaper
   run, not a silent data leak).
   reject / unknown: do not ship. Note in the vault.

Shared structures this pays for
-------------------------------
Freeze before confirmatory (already 02B/05/06). Off-clock MTLF
(already 06). Boundary handles for exogenous telemetry. Claim
status as shipping law. Park licenses "ship this checkpoint."

Do not
------
- Put SGD steps in the CTMC.
- Let AnLF merge weights onto a vertex as if they were load.
- Use the training loss as an intervention claim (association
  is not do(.)).
