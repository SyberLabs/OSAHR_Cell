06  COMPLEX SYSTEM MANAGEMENT
=============================
Grade: PROPOSED. This is the horizontal platform
("network of networks") restated as a
direction that must stay compositional.

What this direction is
----------------------
Manage systems whose topology, rules, and stochastic clocks
co-evolve: RAN + cloud + training cluster + supply of models;
or grid + telecom + logistics. Each subsystem keeps its own
schema. They couple only through typed BoundaryHandles.
OSAHR was built for this composition. GrokCell is the licensed
claim path over the *composite*, not a second kernel per
subsystem.

Facilitation
------------
- Digital Model (SyberRuntime) is how change-control is
  accounted: every intervention is an operation with an
  evaluation. Incident chat is not the RCA database.
- Licensed Network Twin is the first domain pack.
- Training and ML pipelines are domain packs that inject
  payloads (job state, branch heads) into the composite twin.
- Software development is how the packs themselves are
  evolved under debt.

A manager's day
---------------
Open a composite scenario (hurricane / outage / checkpoint
regression). AnLF ports on each boundary. Vault concepts that
are allowed to cross subsystems are few and declared
(critical, outage, prod_data). Junctions are rare and named.
Claim status is scored per physical scenario, not per chat
turn. admit: local legal matches fire. hold: one Brain may
request a vault-legal rewrite in one subsystem at a time.
unknown: withhold, park a note, do not couple subsystems
through chat.

Shared structures this pays for
-------------------------------
Open composition, boundary handles, licensed park, junction-only
semantics, freeze, park. This is the payoff of keeping the
other directions separate: the composite only works if the
kernels were not fused.

Do not
------
- A universal ontology.
- LLM-to-LLM graphs as the safety object (interaction topology
  is the safety object; densify it and you get cascades).
- One residual alpha for the whole composite.
