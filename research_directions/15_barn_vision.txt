The HTML

vision/barn/index.html

What this is
------------
The landowner asks for a shop waitlist. The page may stand.
Email and charges may not, until raise. Crew is on the yard
(lunch = chat). The frame is the coordinator (site, tests,
email, charge). One legal enabled act commits. A mutant that
lives rejects the plate. Park is raising day: send email,
leave charge down. The pack is the record.

That is Cell issue 15 drawn as a picture: specialists, adaptive
network, dynamical system, one-shot, park.

What was deleted before drawing
-------------------------------
- Agent mesh / glowing node graph as the thing that "propagates"
- Dispatcher dashboard / kanban of bots
- Owners as vertices
- A new website the user logs into to run OSAHR
- NetworkBrain workbench as the first SKU
- A second artifact type to look busy
- Automating the raise

"Propagating through the network" in this picture means enabled
joints on the frame changing as timber goes in — resource and
error change who can lift. It does not mean messages hopping
between models.

First principles
----------------
A write that can mail, charge, or ship is not licensed by a
model saying yes. Grok Bot already has crew and a computer. It
does not have a frame. OSAHR is the frame. GrokCell A–E is one
bent (Python module + tests). The barn is the altitude: the
next legal act, including irreversible ones, is selected on the
structure, not by a Chief of Staff.

Algorithm (order kept)
--------------------
1. Questioned. "Fully finished look" is the PI's, to clarify
   vision, not a requirement to ship a UI. "Network" must not
   smuggle in a mesh. "Repair / build until product emerges"
   is admit / reject / hold / park.
2. Deleted the mesh, the dashboard, and the console. If this
   mock is itself too much chrome, delete the stills and keep
   the SVG raise.
3. Simplified to four joints and one example (waitlist).
4. Cycle time: a static storyboard, not a fake live cell.
5. Automate last: Play is a slideshow. It does not run the
   kernel.

Machine
-------
The factory is park on a checkable world (direction 08). This
mock is a sign over the factory door. It is not the factory.
Stay scrappy: one HTML file, four stills, this note.

Communication
-------------
No new jargon. Lunch / frame / raise / pack. Bad news on the
frame (REJECT, HOLD), not in a status channel.

-------------

Branch: Grok Bot as crew; GrokCell as one bent.
