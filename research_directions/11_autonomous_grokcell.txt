11  AUTONOMOUS GROKCELL
=======================
Grade: PROTOTYPE surface. Not confirmatory. Not a kernel change.
Experiment 06 (seed 260826) remains the last executed confirmatory
record.

What this direction is
----------------------
A stateful agentic surface grokbots can run, with intelligent
message passing as a *control plane above them*. OSAHR is the
model-tooling infrastructural layer: the construction twin, DPO
legality, and park. Agents interact through tool ports. They do
not become occurrence types.

This is 08 made concrete as GrokCell, not a specialist swarm.

    Agent -> bus.post(priority, constraints)
          -> admit | hold | reject | unknown
          -> OSAHR assemble-component on admit
          -> sequential Component graph

ODA locks stay in force. Solo is the default. New rail = skill
on MOUTH. oda.spawn is refused in v0 regardless of priority.
Chat is not the database. Park send/publish/delete/sign.

What v0 already does
--------------------
Deterministic bus. Vault-gated classify. Sequential construction
of named components through the kernel. Hold until dependencies
exist. park.request refused unless hold_unresolved and deps are
present. No LLM.

What v0 does not do
-------------------
- Multi-agent routing as a CTMC
- LLM in lambda
- Unfreezing foundation weights (10)
- A confirmatory seed
- python_tests as the checkable world (next slice toward 08.3)

Do not
------
- Invent grokbots to carry messages
- Put bus.kind values into osahr-6g
- Let priority admit an illegal propose
- Call this MEASURED

Viability
---------
This is the right host for grokbots if park stays the join.
It becomes a toy if we spawn a swarm to "orchestrate." The next
honest slice is one checkable artifact type (generated module
plus tests that can kill a mutant), not more owners.
