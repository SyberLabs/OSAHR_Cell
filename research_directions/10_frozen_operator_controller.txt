10  FROZEN OPERATOR + LICENSED z (ENVELOPE EMBEDDING)
====================================================
Grade: PROPOSED platform pattern. Not "OSAHR makes LLMs learn."
Not confirmatory. Depends on 08: no envelope SKU before park is
boring on one checkable world.

What this direction is
----------------------
A frozen foundation model is a large nonlinear operator F_W0.
Do not attempt W_t in R^{|W0|}. Introduce a small adaptive state

    z_t in R^d,    d << |W0|

and (optionally) a typed graph G_t of *task/semantic* structure,
not of Transformer layers. The effective map is

    y_t = F_W0(x_t, z_t)

with z evolving as a hybrid process that OSAHR can audit:

    z-dot = G(z, F_W0(x,z), G_t)     between licensed events
    z+    = J_e(z)                   at a declared occurrence

Training (off clock, 04) may learn a manifold {A_i} so that a
projective modulation

    W_t ~ W0 + sum_i z_i(t) A_i

is a low-rank adapter. At deployment only z moves. The A_i stay
frozen. That is the only computationally honest continuous-
learning claim.

This is envelope embedding (09): F is exogenous. F does not
enter hazards. F is not a hyperedge. F is an AnLF-class operator
on the boundary, or a SyberRuntime Feature, depending on the
artifact.

What OSAHR actually owns
------------------------
    X = (G, B, R, Theta, Z, t, n)

z lives in Theta/Z (constrained AdaptiveParameter / memory),
or in a Liquid descendant field that is *not* the Transformer.
G is vault-legal semantic state (goals, constraints, uncertainty,
memory nodes) as in 06. R enable/disable of *modulation ports*
is legal meta-rewriting. Chat is not G.

Junction law (05) licenses a z-update the same way it licenses
a rewrite: admit / hold_unresolved / reject / outcome_unknown.
A chat preference is not an estimand. Without a held-out suite
that can kill a mutant, status is outcome_unknown and park
refuses.

Why 02A is the warning, not the demo
------------------------------------
The loop z -> F -> consequences -> z is the same recursion 02A
MEASURED on (H, lambda, G). Closing it can amplify misspecification
while remaining well-defined. Therefore:

- no LLM inside lambda
- no silent z-update on every token
- intervention claims on F(x,z) vs F(x,z0) need a declared
  estimand and an ensemble, as in 02B/05
- "the agent is the frozen model plus a liquid organism" is
  allowed as architecture only if Stabilize of z is park, not
  context stuffing

Capacity question worth asking later
------------------------------------
How much behavioral class B_d is reachable by dim(z)=d around a
frozen F, on a *checkable* artifact (patch, routing-policy file,
claim text with a verifier). Not on open chat. This is a research
question for after 08 gate 3, not a reason to start an LLM SKU.

Relation to other directions
----------------------------
04 already said: govern training, do not be PyTorch; SGD is off
clock. 03 already said: Feature incurs Test debt; the generator
does not discharge it. 08 already said: one artifact type until
dull. 10 is those three with F_W0 as the generator and z as the
only online degree of freedom. It is not a fourth kernel.

Do not
------
- Pitch "continual retraining of the LLM."
- Put tokens, layers, or attention heads in the 6G schema.
- Let z rewrite G.
- Use next-token loss as do(.).
- Activate adapters as if they were route-task matches.
- Start this before park is boring on python_tests (08).

Viability
---------
High as the *envelope* pattern once a checkable world exists
(same park as 03/04). Rejected as an LLM product. Crowded, and
02A already showed the closed loop is the hazard. The system
around the Transformer may become liquid. The Transformer does
not. The Cell does not become a chat engine.
