# Research notes: Experiment 04

03 showed that 2 s single-query calibration barely identified T. 04 asked whether matching horizon and estimand support is enough.

It is enough to **identify** cells. It is not enough to **transport** them to a new seed at n=5 scenarios per regime. That is consistent with 02B's own warning that five-scenario bootstrap intervals are scenario-sensitivity statements, not population effects.

The 3 s horizon still leaves many oracle Δ=0 trajectories. A trust field cannot be more informative than the contrast it is fitted on.
